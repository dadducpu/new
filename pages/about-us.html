About Us

We're more than just developers—we're storytellers, problem-solvers, and digital artisans.

Our Journey

Started in 2018 as a small duo, Grain & Mortar has grown into an award-winning digital agency. What began with coffee conversations about design and code is now a team of passionate creators dedicated to transforming businesses through exceptional digital experiences.

The Beginning

2018-2019: Two friends with shared visions launched their first project—a portfolio site for a local photographer. Their unique blend of aesthetic sensibility and technical skill quickly gained recognition in the design community.

Growth & Recognition

2019-2021: Word-of-mouth referrals grew their client base. They won their first "Agency of the Year" award and expanded their team, taking on larger corporate projects.

Expansion

2021-2023: Grain & Mortar opened offices in two cities, established international clients across multiple industries, and launched their proprietary design system.

Today

We're now a team of 25+ creatives serving clients worldwide, from startups to Fortune 500 companies. Our philosophy remains unchanged: quality over quantity, soul over scale.
