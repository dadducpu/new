How to Apply

We're committed to a fair, transparent hiring process that respects everyone's time.

1

Submit Your Application

Send us your resume and a brief introduction about why you'd be great at Grain & Mortar.

2

The Interview Process

We'll reach out within a week. Expect a series of interviews, including team discussions and case work.

3

The Offer

We'll provide a detailed offer within two weeks of your final interview.

4

Welcome Aboard!

We'll have you onboarding within a week of accepting the offer.

Quick Tips for Your Application:

  • Show, don't just tell—include links to your portfolio or projects
  • Be specific about how you'd contribute to Grain & Mortar's work
  • Mention any passion projects or creative pursuits outside of work
  • We value diverse perspectives and backgrounds
