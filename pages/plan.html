Pricing Questions

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?

Yes! We're committed to supporting social good and offer a 25% discount on all services for registered nonprofit organizations.

What's included in maintenance?

Maintenance includes security updates, backups, bug fixes, performance monitoring, and content updates. Additional features may incur extra charges.

Can I make changes after launch?

Absolutely! We offer monthly maintenance plans that include routine updates and bug fixes. Major feature additions or redesigns are billed separately.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we work with many clients on milestone-based payments. Contact us to discuss your specific situation.

What happens if I don't renew my maintenance plan?

Your website will continue functioning, but we'll no longer be able to provide proactive support. Security updates and backups may become more limited.
